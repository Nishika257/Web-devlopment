About Honeybees

Honeybees are small insects that live together in large groups called colonies. They are famous for making honey and helping flowers grow by carrying pollen from one flower to another.

Why Are Honeybees Important?

Honeybees are important pollinators. When they visit flowers to collect nectar, pollen sticks to their bodies. They carry this pollen to other flowers and help plants produce fruits and seeds.

Life Inside a Beehive

A honeybee colony has a queen bee, worker bees, and drone bees. Worker bees collect food, protect the hive, and take care of young bees. The queen bee lays eggs and helps the colony grow.

How Do Bees Make Honey?

Worker bees collect sweet nectar from flowers and carry it back to the hive. The nectar is stored inside honeycomb cells. Over time, it becomes the delicious honey that bees use as food.

Protect the Honeybees 🌼

We can help honeybees by growing flowering plants, keeping gardens clean, and protecting their natural habitats. Healthy bees help create a healthy environment for everyone.
