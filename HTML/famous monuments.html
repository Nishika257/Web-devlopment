Let's explore two famous monuments from around the world.

1. Taj Mahal

India

The Taj Mahal is a beautiful white marble monument located in Agra, India.

It was built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

The Taj Mahal is one of the Seven Wonders of the World.

Learn more about the Taj Mahal

2. Eiffel Tower

France

The Eiffel Tower is a famous landmark located in Paris, France.

It was completed in 1889 and was designed by Gustave Eiffel's company.

Millions of people visit the Eiffel Tower every year.
